# Traverse Analysis — Slow-Object Surfacing Review

Review brief: make the analysis better at surfacing and confirming SLOW, mundane
explanations (balloon, lantern, bird, debris, distant aircraft misjudged as near),
while never making a fast, far, or maneuvering solution UNREACHABLE. Section 4
("What not to do") records proposals rejected precisely because they would have
biased the analysis toward slow answers; it is as load-bearing as the plan itself.

---

Synthesis of 47 verified findings across six lenses (slow-surfacing, foreclosure, accuracy, missing-hypothesis, UX, performance), plus a partial first-pass plan retained for its rejection reasoning. I re-verified the load-bearing citations in the working tree myself; corrections are noted inline.

**One structural fact governs the whole review.** Range is the weakly-observable direction, and *three independent mechanisms* all push the solution toward the sensor: the closed-form fits' metric objective (§H1), the unfloored CV seed (§H1), and the QP's unfloored dive (§H2). Meanwhile exactly *one* mechanism pushes the other way — the jet-speed prior in the sweep score. The system is not neutrally calibrated between slow and fast; it has a near-field attractor that the code documents in three separate comments and defends against in only one caller. Every item below is positioned against that.

---

## 1. The headline

**H1 — The closed-form fits minimise perpendicular distance in *metres*, and the one seed that feeds the whole Monte Carlo family is run without the guard the solver's own comment demands.**
`src/LOSFitting.js:150-166` builds normal-equation rows from the metric projector `(I − dd^T)`; `:203-207` reports the residual via `_pointToRayDistance` (`:67-72`), in metres. That objective decreases monotonically as the trajectory scales toward the sensor, bottoming out at the exact-zero sensor-path solution — documented verbatim at `src/LOSFitting.js:80-88`: *"the sensor's own path is a zero-residual solution whenever the sensor flies a CV/CA-representable trajectory."* The guard exists (`dataset.minRange`, `:88`, `:105-106`) and `grep -n minRange` returns exactly **one** caller that sets it: `src/AnalyzeTraverse.js:838` (`minRange: 500`, noise floor). `prepareSweep` at `src/AnalyzeTraverse.js:157-160` builds its seed `ds` with `maxRange: null` and **no `minRange`**, floors `rangeEstimates` at `Math.max(1, …)` (`:176-180`), and that seed sets the sampling window for all ten Monte Carlo tiles (`lambda = est*(0.9 + rng()*0.2)`, `LOSFitting.js:451-452`, `:660-662`).
*Why now:* it is the purest form of the failure the mandate names — a manufactured near/slow answer wearing ten independent-looking fits — and the fix is one property on one object, matching a caller that already exists 660 lines away.

**H2 — `minDist: 120` is inert in the primary slow-vs-fast sweep; the QP dives and the whole score surface is read off the dive.**
`src/TraverseAnalysis.js:1020-1021`: `minDist = options.minDist ?? 120` but `useFloor = options.rangeFloor ?? false`. `grep -n rangeFloor` returns exactly one setter — `:1487` (`fitPlausibleBestRange`). The two hot call sites, `sweepConstAirSpeed:772` and `constAirSpeedTrack:902`, pass `minDist: 120` and no `rangeFloor`, so the floor rows at `:1109-1113` never fire. The comment at `:768-770` claims *"minDist keeps the QP's range-on-ray positive"* — it does not.
*Why now:* `meanAngularError` catches the pathological collapse, but not the moderate dive — lambda falling to a few hundred metres while staying positive — and `startDist`, speed, and the sweep's entire score surface are read off the unfloored solution. Two characters at each of two sites.

**H3 — The slow range profile has no search of its own; it inherits a bracket the *fast* objective chose.**
`src/AnalyzeTraverse.js:2534` sets `resolvedRanges = sweep.ranges`; `:2542-2547` (fast) and `:2549-2553` (slow) both consume it. `sweepConstAirSpeed` expands geometrically past a touched edge (`src/TraverseAnalysis.js:803-836`, 200 m floor at `:826`) — but that expansion is gated on the sweep's own score, which carries `score += 0.2*((speedMs − speedTarget)/speedSigma)²` (`:779`). `rangeProfile` (`:1390-1430`) computes `boundarySides` at `:1426-1427` and **never appends**.
*Why now:* the slow family gets a footnote (`boundaryLimited` → `incomplete` → demotion, `TraverseRanking.js:228-229`) but never a search. The correction to the original finding matters: the near field *is* reachable, but only if a jet-speed-weighted score happens to walk there.

**H4 — Wind agreement — the only non-circular balloon confirmation — is computed twice and reduced to nothing.**
`windProfileComparisonHTML` (`src/AnalyzeTraverse.js:3798-3846`, docstring calls itself *"the balloon plausibility bridge"*) samples the free fit's sheared wind at 7 altitudes against `wf.sampleWindAtAltitude`, formats `<td>`s, retains no scalar. One call site, `:3890`. `grep -n wind src/TraverseRanking.js` → one hit, line 35, a category description string. `buildVerdict` (`:1805-1832`, read in full) compares only `errDeg` and then explicitly disclaims that residuals identify the object (`:1817-1819`) — leaving no positive evidence in its place.
*Why now:* this is the difference between "a balloon can be made to fit" and "a balloon is confirmed." Every ingredient exists.

**H5 — Soft priors displace the solution but are structurally invisible in the number the tile prints.**
`SkyLanternModel.js:148-153` adds `0.5*(spd/10)²` with no wind prior; `:156` adds a negative-shear penalty; `QuadcopterModel.js:133-135` adds `0.3*(wspd/6)²` **unconditionally**, because `AnalyzeTraverse.js:2697` constructs `new QuadcopterModel()` bare while `:2680-2682` wires the lantern; `FixedWingModel.js:119-134` applies turn/climb/cruise penalties with no fallback at all. Cost is `errDeg/errSigma + extraCost` with `errSigma = 0.02` (`LOSFitting.js:1357`, `:1309`). **The load-bearing part, verified at `LOSFitting.js:1478-1498`:** the reported `errDeg` is recomputed as pure `_angularError` over all frames, deliberately excluding `extraCost`. Meanwhile the tile says the wind is *"INFERRED, not assumed"* (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:1120-1122`).
*Why now:* the priors are not merely undeclared in prose — they are unobservable in the reported metric. That is a correctness-of-disclosure defect independent of whether the priors are well-chosen.

**H6 — The broad screen is a ceiling filter; nothing computes a positive mundane statement, and the comparator ranks "complete" above "plausible."**
`TraverseRanking.js:190-193` — all four branches are upper bounds (`gMax > 9 || speedMaxKt > 900 || err > 0.5` …), so a 5 kt drifter and a 640 kt jet both reach rank 3. `completenessBadges` (`:412-419`) emits five badges, all cautions, no positive. And the comparator at `:266-272` (verified verbatim) places `incomplete` **before** `rank`: a complete rank-0 "Implausible" tile sorts above a kinematically mild rank-3 tile whose only flaw is honestly reporting a boundary touch.
*Why now:* on agua-class scenes the slow families are the ones that most often reach a search edge (broad, weakly-constrained range), so in practice honesty is penalised exactly where the mundane answer lives. The fix is symmetric — Minimum Acceleration (the fast/far tile, `boundaryLimited` written at `AnalyzeTraverse.js:992`) gains equally.

**H7 — There is no angular-size channel, and the balloon forward model has never been closed-loop validated.**
`grep -niE "angularSize|apparentSize|subtend|angularDiam"` across `AnalyzeTraverse.js`, `LOSFitting.js`, `TraverseAnalysis.js`, `TraverseRanking.js`, `TraverseAnalysisData.js`, all three `*Model.js`: **zero hits**. `buildAnalysisDataset` packs only `S`, `D`, `W` (`TraverseAnalysisData.js:52-54`, `:93-94`), while `CObjectTracking.calculateWeightedCentroid` (`src/CObjectTracking.js:550`) already walks the ROI per-pixel with a positive weight function (`:612-615`) whose second moment *is* the angular extent. Separately, `src/BalloonPhysics.js` is a real buoyancy/drag integrator imported by exactly two files (`src/nodes/CNodeBalloonTrack.js:20`, `tests/BalloonPhysics.test.js:13`) and **never** by `SkyLanternModel` or `fitPhysicsModel`.
*Why now:* angular size is the only cheap observable that breaks the range degeneracy, and it constrains far/fast exactly as hard as near/slow. And the closed loop — generate a balloon with known physics, run the fit, does it recover range and wind? — converts every argument in this document into a number, needs no browser, and never touches the 15-minute pipeline.

## 2. Tiered plan

### T1 — high value, low risk, do first

**T1.1 — Closed-loop balloon recovery test (Jest).** *New `tests/TraverseBalloonRecovery.test.js`.*
Use `integrateBalloonPositions` (`src/BalloonPhysics.js`) to synthesize a truth track with known wind/shear/range; build a dataset in the shape `buildAnalysisDataset` returns (`TraverseAnalysisData.js:93`); run `fitPhysicsModel(ds, new Set(), new SkyLanternModel(), opts)` (`LOSFitting.js:1276`); assert recovered mean range, wind vector, altitude.
*Mechanism:* measures the three magnitudes this whole review can only argue about — the calm-prior displacement in metres, the range-grid effect, and how much discriminating power a wind-agreement statistic actually carries. Run it with the prior on and off; that number decides T1.4's threshold.
*Risk:* none — pure test, no pipeline. *Size:* ~150 lines.
Note `tests/SkyLanternModel.test.js:222-227` already concedes *"LOS-only data admits a degenerate closer-and-slower / farther-and-faster family… the model guarantees the REGIME, not the exact member"* and accepts 3–25 kt against a 6.7 m/s truth. That tolerance is what you are trying to tighten. **Report the displacement in metres, not pass/fail.**

**T1.2 — Set `minRange: 500` on the `prepareSweep` CV seed.** `src/AnalyzeTraverse.js:157-160`.
Add `minRange: 500` to the seed `ds`, matching the *existing documented caller* at `:838`. Also return `median` and p5/p95 of `rangeEstimates` and interpolate them into the MC tile note at `:1534-1536`, so *"0.9x to 1.1x of the constant-velocity fit"* names the actual seed.
*Mechanism:* removes the sensor-path collapse from the seed that windows all ten MC tiles. **Judgement on a real conflict:** the foreclosure lens proposed `minRange = ranges[0]`; that is `max(0.3 NM, 0.1 × anchorDist)` ≈ 3.7 km at a 20 NM anchor — a brand-new near-side foreclosure in exactly the direction that matters most here. Reject it. 500 m matches an existing precedent and sits below every model's own floor.
*Risk:* low; changes MC seed on scenes where the CV fit was collapsing, which is the point. Add a `failures` entry when the seed is rejected. *Size:* small.

**T1.3 — Add `rangeFloor: true` at `TraverseAnalysis.js:772` and `:902`.**

*Risk:* the 120 m soft floor with ×8 escalation effectively pins lambda ≥ 120 m, so document it as a **declared prior** (see F3). No effect on far/fast. *Size:* small.

**T1.4 — Disclose the priors instead of removing them.**

*Mechanism:* makes "inferred, not assumed" either true or qualified, and closes the `errDeg`-excludes-`extraCost` gap at `LOSFitting.js:1478-1498`.
*Risk:* none — display only, no solution moves; assert `errDeg` is unchanged. *Size:* small.
**Do not** flatten the calm prior below 15 m/s — §4.

**T1.5 — Fix the comparator: `rank → eligible → incomplete → activePins → secondaryScore → errDeg`.** `TraverseRanking.js:266-272`.
Demote `incomplete` from ordering key to badge; it already renders one at `:414`.
*Mechanism:* stops a complete-but-implausible tile outranking a plausible-but-honest one. Symmetric — helps Minimum Speed and Minimum Acceleration equally.
*Risk:* leading with `rank` promotes the hardcoded caps at `:190-193` to dominant key, which pins a genuinely anomalous high-g candidate to the bottom of its group *by construction*. Mitigate by keeping tier caps as display grouping only. **T1.6 — Guard `heading` at near-zero air speed, at 0.05 m/s not 0.5.** `TraverseAnalysis.js:102`, `:127-132`.
`heading[f] = atan2(va[0], va[1])` has no magnitude guard, though the identical guard exists 150 lines away in `compareTrackToTruth` (`:251`, documented at `:195-196`). *Risk:* touches a shared search objective, so it can change which cell the sweep selects. A/B both testbeds. *Size:* small.

Not headroom for a wider grid (the grid is policy, `TraverseAnalysis.js:919-936`), but a 15-minute cycle is itself a barrier to the sensitivity testing the report's own text demands (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:1834-1835`, `:3757`).

### T2 — high value, needs care

**T2.1 — Give `rangeProfile` its own bidirectional expansion.** `TraverseAnalysis.js:1390-1430`, called at `AnalyzeTraverse.js:2549-2553`.
Mirror `sweepConstAirSpeed:803-836` — same geometric schedule (`lo / 2.5^(i/8)`, 200 m floor), keyed on `out.boundarySides` not raw argmin. Pass `expand: rangeIsDefault` for the slow profile. Independently, **union** a fixed log-spaced near-field tail (~12 points, 0.2 NM up to `loNM`) onto `adaptiveRangeList`'s output (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:2977-2987`) rather than re-spacing it.
*Mechanism:* the slow family votes on whether the near field is worth sampling, instead of inheriting a jet-weighted decision. Expansion is bidirectional, so no far answer is foreclosed.
*Risk — three, all real.* (i) Low-end expansion walks toward the zero-range attractor; the 200 m floor and the slow profile's speed prior (`vTarget 5 kt, vSigma 20 kt`, `AnalyzeTraverse.js:2548`) are the only things suppressing it — do not lower either in the same commit. (ii) Appending rows perturbs heatmap index order; re-sort and recompute `familyLoIndex`/`familyHiIndex` together (`TraverseAnalysis.js:1416-1424`). (iii) **`slowValleyContrast` (`:1974-1981`) and `describeRangeConvergence.contrast` (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:3200-3221`) are row-count statistics with no range weighting**, so *any* change to grid population silently re-tunes the slow/fast regime gate at `:2005-2011`. Making them spacing-invariant (integrate over log-range) must land in the same commit. *Size:* medium.

**T2.2 — Wind agreement as a stated number, gated four ways.** `AnalyzeTraverse.js:3804-3846` → `hypothesisStats` + one `buildVerdict` sentence.
Derive it from the **existing 7-row altitude profile** (`:3810-3814`), not from a single-altitude `windPrior` sample (`:2624-2630`) — the shear is the actual discriminator and collapsing to one number loses it. Attach `params.windAgreement = {dSpeedKt, dDirDeg, verdict}` via the existing `aspectVerdict` machinery (`TraverseRanking.js:336-340`); keep the table, the scalar is a pointer to it.
Four mandatory gates, each of which *inverts* the value if omitted:
- **Free-fit only.** Both variants share `key: "lantern"` (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:1117`, `:1127`) — and the comment at `:1110-1113` shows this is **deliberate**, for group/apply-path sharing. So do not split the key; add an explicit `windPolicy`-based discriminator. Computing agreement on the *pinned* fit compares a drift the cost function already pulled to within `windPriorSigma = 7.7 m/s` (`SkyLanternModel.js:64`, `:141-147`) against a ~4 m/s threshold — a confirmation with zero information content.
- **Measured provenance only.** `windPrior` deliberately does *not* exclude `source === "manual"` (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:2586-2589`, `:2629`), while `windProfileComparisonHTML` does (`:3816-3817`). A badge off a hand-set constant reports the analyst's own assumption back as confirmation.
- **Non-calm only.** Require the measured field's mean speed to exceed ~2× tolerance, or a near-stationary near-camera solution earns "matches measured wind" from two vectors both ≈ 0.
- **Negative form loud.** It would be the first *affirmative* badge in a set of five cautions (`TraverseRanking.js:412-419`), and only a lantern can earn it. Put the positive statement in `hypothesisStats` and prose; emit only the *inconsistent* form as a badge.
Also fix `buildVerdict`'s `withTrack.find(h => h.key === "lantern")` (`:1806`), which deterministically returns the free variant pushed first at `:1117`. Select **by wind policy, not by argmin residual** — "else lowest errDeg" is an argmin over two balloon variants that flatters the slow side. Report both residuals with their policies, `lanternFree ?? lanternPinned` as fallback.
*Risk:* the free fit's calm prior (H5) biases inferred wind low, so this under-reports agreement on windy days until T1.4 lands. Ship T1.4, T2.2 together. **Do not let it touch `rank` or `secondaryScore`.** *Size:* medium.

**T2.3 — Residual structure and tail, plus hold-out.** `TraverseAnalysis.js:170-183`, display at `TraverseRanking.js:309-321`.
`meanAngularError` returns a bare scalar; `grep -nE "maxErr|p95|percentile|rmsErr"` → zero hits; `crossValid|holdout|trainFrac|predictive` → zero hits. Two additions: (a) accumulate `p95`, `max`, and the sign-change count / lag-1 autocorrelation of the along-track residual in the same loop; (b) fit on `[f0, fmid]`, score on `[fmid, f1]` using the existing `abFrameRange` plumbing (`TraverseAnalysisData.js:120-142`), for `FORWARD_KEYS` only at first (`TraverseRanking.js:51`).
*Mechanism:* a structured residual (slow sinusoid) is the signature of a *wrong model* and today scores identically to white noise of the same mean. Hold-out is the direct antidote to residual/truth divergence: over-flexible fitters collapse, a correct low-DOF physical model does not. It is also the honest strength test for the balloon — an 8-parameter `SkyLanternModel` (`:78-87`) that *predicts* held-out frames is evidence; one that interpolates its training window is not.
*Risk:* hold-out doubles fit cost on top of a 15-minute run — hence T1.7 first. Report only; ranking on it is a separate decision. *Size:* medium.

**T2.4 — Downsample `rangeProfile`'s QP — but gate it as a science change.** `AnalyzeTraverse.js:2542-2553` → `TraverseAnalysis.js:1390-1396`.
Both `rangeProfile` calls omit `targetN` and `iters`, so they solve at full resolution with `iters` defaulting to 6 (`:1017`), while the sweep they feed downsamples at `:760` and passes `iters: 3`. ~3× frames × 2× iterations for ≥88 solves.
**This is the one performance item that is a science change wearing a performance costume.** `slowProfile` feeds `slowValleyContrast` and `computeSaddle` (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:459, 499, 507`), and the slow branch is gated on contrast ≥ 2.5 **and** `slowScore < 0.8 × fastScore` (`TraverseAnalysis.js:2005-2011`) — so any change to the score curve can silently flip whether the Constant Air Speed tile reads slow or fast. Worse, downsampling recomputes `accelScale = fps²/G_ACCEL/(hA*hA)` (`:1025`) at reduced fps, so the acceleration penalty is **reweighted**, not merely denoised.
Add explicit `targetN`; downsample only the QP solve and linearly upsample `lam` before `trackMetrics` (mirroring `constAirSpeedTrack:899-916`); **clamp the stride so the interpolation segment stays shorter than `trackMetrics`' differencing half-window** `h = max(1, floor(max(3, round(0.5*fps))/2))` (`:66-67`, `:84`), else g-load and airSpeed drop spuriously.
*Gate:* require the score curve to move within a stated tolerance on **both** testbeds; if either branch moves materially, ship as an analyst-visible toggle, not a silent default. *Size:* small to write, medium to validate.

Separately, move `fitGroundVehicle` out of the `groundMode === "On the ground"` conditional (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:1563`; `GROUND_MODES` at `:242-249` has four modes, default `"Airborne (any)"` at `:230`, so the boat/car tile is withheld in **three of four**). *Risk (the one to fence):* the ground candidate is a zero-free-parameter exact ray/shell intersection, so `errDeg ≈ 0` by construction, **and** `groundVehicle` is in `RAY_KEYS` (`TraverseRanking.js:52`) so `effectiveErrDeg` subtracts a further `RAY_SOLVER_ALLOWANCE_DEG`. ## 4. What not to do

**Do not flatten or remove the calm-wind prior below 15 m/s.** It lets the free lantern fit invent ~29 m/s of wind at zero cost, lowering the balloon's residual and making the slow explanation rank *better* than the evidence supports. It also destroys the free-vs-pinned diagnostic that the two-variant design (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:2657`, `:2670-2690`) exists to provide: if the free fit selects any wind gratis, "the inferred wind disagrees with the sounding" stops being evidence. Ship the disclosure (T1.4); treat recalibration as a separate decision backed by winds-aloft climatology.

**Do not pass `minRange = ranges[0]` to the CV seed.** That is `max(0.3 NM, 0.1 × anchorDist)` ≈ 3.7 km at a 20 NM anchor — a new near-side foreclosure in the exact direction that matters most. Use 500 m (T1.2).

**Do not replace `adaptiveRangeList` with a fixed 44-point log grid.** All verifiers refuted the arithmetic. On agua it is *worse in the slow band*: today 0.3–8 NM linear = 0.179 NM/step; proposed ≈ 0.22–0.27 NM/step at 2 NM. On Gimbal it is 3.5–9× coarser in the far field. And because `familyBand` (`TraverseAnalysis.js:868-873`) and the `familyCount/familyTotal` prose (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:3654-3655`, `:3716-3721`) are counted in **grid rows**, a coarser far grid mechanically makes fast answers read as *more ambiguous*. Union a tail instead (T2.1).

**Do not lower the 15 kt speed floor or make the near-field floors user-lowerable.** Both are currently *accidental guards* against the zero-range degenerate family. The QP objective improves monotonically as range → 0, `straightFlightScore`'s absolute m/s² terms (`TraverseAnalysis.js:694-702`) are not scale-aware, and `spdErr` (`:779-780`, `spdFidSigma = 10 kt`) rewards near-camera cells once slow speeds are admitted. A 5 m `minObjectRange` setting yields a degenerate near-camera tile that outscores everything. Emit a *note* when a floor is reached (T1.3, F3); do not thread a control into it.

**Do not compute `slowValleyContrast` over a fixed 0.2–90 NM band to open the slow regime gate.** That adds low-g, low-speed-prior rows which depress `min`, raising contrast *most* on exactly the range-unobservable scenes the gate was built to catch (its docstring at `TraverseAnalysis.js:1965-1972` says so). It would open the gate using the known zero-information degeneracy. Better: require the slow profile's family band to be **interior** (`familyLoIndex > 0 && familyHiIndex < length-1`) — an evidence test that cannot be inflated by widening the grid. Best value here is simply *disclosing* `slowGateSpeed`, `slowValleyContrast`, and `slowScore/fastScore` with thresholds named.

**Do not widen `boundaryLimited` to "winner in the outermost 2 samples" against a fixed near floor.** With a 0.2 NM floor a *genuine* near-field balloon at 0.25 NM would be branded search-incomplete and made ineligible (`TraverseRanking.js:228-229`). The family-touch test already exists (`TraverseAnalysis.js:858-867`).

**Do not emit a positive wind badge without all four gates in T2.2.** Ungated it fires on the wind-*pinned* tile (shared key), where `windPriorSigma = 7.7 m/s` guarantees agreement inside any 4 m/s threshold — zero information content. Ungated on provenance it badges a hand-set wind as measured agreement, which fires on testbed A immediately.

**Do not split the two lanterns' `key`.** The shared key is deliberate and documented (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:1110-1113`) — it shares the forward-model group, apply path, and prose. Discriminate on `windPolicy` instead.

**Do not screen on implied physical diameter.** Report only, as an upper bound (T3.4). Blooming makes extent over-estimated; a hard gate becomes a new foreclosure that indicts far solutions spuriously.

**Do not add per-class kinematic envelopes as hard screens.** The ladder at `TraverseRanking.js:190-193` is object-type-agnostic, which is a real defect — but replacing it with per-class limits ("a balloon can't pull 3 g") is precisely how you make a maneuvering-drone answer unreachable. Make it a *reason string* on the specific hypothesis, never a rank cap.

**Do not add a slow-object term to the sweep score to "balance" `speedTarget` (`TraverseAnalysis.js:779`).** Two opposed undeclared priors is worse than one. Expose the existing one; do not stack.

**Do not pin the quadcopter's wind to `windPrior` as a shortcut.** It replaces the free-wind drone with a wind-pinned one and *deletes* the free-wind hypothesis, swapping a soft `w²/120` penalty for a stiff `w²/25` pin (4.8× tighter). And it **backfires on testbed A**, whose hand-set wind is 277° @ **0 kn** — pinning would enforce calm air, making the slow bias worse. If a wind-pinned drone is wanted, add it as a *second* fit, as `lantern`/`lanternMeasured` coexist.

**Do not remove the astro magnitude cap.** At mag < 11 there are ~115k catalogue stars, ~2.8 per square degree, so the nearest star to an arbitrary direction is ~0.3° by chance, and `TraverseRanking.js:161-165` promotes anything under 0.1° with `visible !== false`. That manufactures "it's a star" for essentially any sightline.

**Do not add a "camera-locked optical artifact" candidate tile.** As specified it would announce that verdict with maximum confidence on **every run using the default `"Camera Center"` LOS source** (`data/custom/SitCustom.js:651`), because `D` and the proposed boresight `C` are the same vector. Its discriminator (off-boresight angle constant while boresight swings) is *also* what a tracker slaved to a genuine object produces. Instead extend the existing `losProvenance` block (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:3050-3086`) with one line computed only when `selectedIsCameraCenter` (`:3063`) is false, and print "not available — vacuous" when it is true.

**Do not build a periodicity/wingbeat test yet.** `CNodeTrackingOverlay` interpolates sparse manual keyframes with Catmull-Rom (`src/nodes/CNodeTrackingOverlay.js:548-790`), so a periodogram measures *knot spacing* and would print "consistent with flapping flight" from an artifact of the analyst's own clicking. Nyquist is set by `dataset.fps` after resampling, not video fps, so a real wingbeat can fold to a false low-frequency peak.

**Do not coarsen DE's integration step via `max(model.maxDt, T/2000)`.** It is a *no-op for the lantern* (`SkyLanternModel.js:52`, `maxDt = 0.25` already exceeds any plausible T/2000) and degrades **only** the maneuvering models (`QuadcopterModel.js:43`, `FixedWingModel.js:33`, `maxDt = 1/30`) — asymmetric pressure toward the mundane answer. Likewise do not cut Nelder-Mead `maxIter`: NM *does* have a convergence stop (`NelderMead.js:98`), it fires around iteration ~2,720, and truncating left a solved lantern range 492 m off truth. Only DE lacks a stagnation break; if you want one, make it a genuine no-improvement-over-N-generations break with the tolerance stated in the UI.

**Do not accept T2.4 on an "accept only if slow gets closer to truth" criterion.** That is a one-way ratchet toward slow: it rejects the change precisely when the change is correctly removing a spurious near/slow bias.

**Do not let gallery "set aside" clicks reshape the verdict.** `dismissed` (`AnalyzeTraverse.js:4483`) is cosmetic today, and the tempting fix is wrong: `buildVerdict`'s leader sentences are the bolded, quotable content of a shareable artefact (`:3090-3103`), and letting unlogged clicks reshape them converts a mechanical statement into a rhetorical one — a whole group can vanish if all members are set aside. **Mark, never hide.** The defensible floor is relabelling the button's `title`/`aria-label` ("exclude from consideration", `:4565-4570`) to match the separator text at `:4500-4503`, which already correctly says the tiles are "still ranked as they were."

**Do not ship the IRLS inverse-range reweighting as drafted.** Three blocking problems: at the degenerate solution every perpendicular offset is exactly zero, so the objective is zero under *any* finite weights; `w = 1/max(lambda, rangeFloor)` gives every sub-floor frame a *constant* weight, reintroducing the collapse; and row scaling against the fixed `PENALTY = 1e4` (`LOSFitting.js:89`, applied to the unscaled `lambdaRow` at `:120-125`) turns the soft bounds into near-hard constraints, including the `maxRange` clamp at `:110-112`, pinning far/fast solutions at their ceiling. Sequence: make `PENALTY` scale-relative first, then trial reweighting behind a flag, and never silently mutate the existing "Global Fit: CV/CA" tiles.

**Do not "mark the knee" on the polynomial order sweep, or reorder it by `mcOrder`.** Per the tile's own text a degree-*k* curve buys only ~⌊k/2⌋ direction changes, so a genuinely maneuvering target legitimately needs high order and a knee marker reads as a verdict against it. And reordering clobbers truth mode, where mean 3D separation *is* the intended key (`TraverseRanking.js:261-265`).

**Do not tune thresholds against testbed A's Spearman rho.** Testbed A is a *single synthetic orbiting generator* with a hand-set 277° @ 0 kn wind. The moment a screening-vs-truth agreement statistic exists, the temptation is to tune `TraverseRanking.js:190` and `straightFlightScore` weights until it looks good on one object class — baking an orbit-shaped prior into a general tool. Ship it explicitly labelled non-objective.

**Do not rank on wind agreement, hold-out residual, residual structure, or implied size.** All should be *reported* for at least one release cycle and validated against T1.1 before any touches `rank` or `secondaryScore`.
