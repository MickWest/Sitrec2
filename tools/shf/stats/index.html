Starlink Flare Statistics

Open the output of flare-stats.mjs to see its charts.

Choose the output folder (for example flare-stats-out), or drop its files on this page. The page reads the files in your browser and uploads nothing.

To load a folder on this site instead, add ?data=<folder path>/ to this page's address.

Each count is the flares seen by one observer at that latitude, in the whole sky above the horizon. It is not a worldwide total. All counts every glint inside the 5° flare cone. Visible counts the glints that at least double the satellite's normal brightness. Sky conditions (twilight, haze, the Moon, light pollution) are not included.
